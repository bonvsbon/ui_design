# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Thailand looking for casual footwear who want to choose a pair by style or everyday activity. They may browse on mobile or desktop and need to compare sandals, slides, and sneakers without already knowing a model name.

## Product Purpose

Create a product-led Gambol brand website that introduces the brand, helps visitors find relevant footwear, and offers a small interactive shopping flow. The prototype should make it easy to explore models, inspect product details, try a local demo bag, and continue to Gambol's official online store to buy.

## Brand Personality

Bright, colorful, and practical. The experience should feel approachable and energetic while keeping product details concrete and easy to scan. The official Gambol website supplied by the user is the primary reference.

## Anti-references

No separate anti-reference was specified. Keep the experience recognizably connected to Gambol's product and technology cues; avoid importing an unrelated luxury or editorial brand style.

## Design Principles

- Put real Gambol products at the center of browsing.
- Help visitors choose by product type and everyday use, not only by model code.
- Explain G-BOLD benefits with specific product information such as softness, lightness, and durability.
- Keep the path from discovery to the official store clear; label demo shopping behavior honestly.
- Use color and motion to express Gambol's lively identity without getting in the way of reading or choosing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear text contrast, semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. These defaults were accepted by the user; no additional accessibility requirements were specified.
