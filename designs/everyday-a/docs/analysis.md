# Step 1 — Analysis: REEF (design reference) × GAMBOL (content source)

> REEF is used **only** as a reference for UX patterns and rhythm. No REEF layout, copy, colour, logo or component is reproduced.

## 1. What REEF does well (worth adapting as *patterns*)

| Pattern observed on reef.com | Why it works | How GAMBOL adapts it (originally) |
|---|---|---|
| Thin promo bar ("free shipping / easy returns") | Removes purchase anxiety before the first scroll | Thai-language, CMS-driven rotating messages (free shipping ฿599, store pick-up, new season) |
| Gender-split entry point (Women's / Men's) right in the hero | Shoppers self-select in one tap | Hero CTAs `SHOP MEN` / `SHOP WOMEN`, plus a 4-up `FIND YOUR PAIR` grid that also covers Kids and Sneakers |
| Category browsing as a horizontal strip of thumbnails | Fast scanning of many categories on mobile | Lifestyle photo cards (not product cut-outs) that scroll horizontally on mobile and sit as an asymmetric grid on desktop |
| "New for Women / New for Men" product carousels | Keeps newness visible without a separate page | Two alternating editorial blocks: campaign photo + 4 products, mirrored left/right for rhythm |
| Full-width collection campaigns between product rows | Breaks catalogue monotony, sells a mood | `MADE FOR EVERYDAY MOVEMENT` brand story, `SUMMER ESCAPE` featured collection, product-story section |
| Short, benefit-led product copy (one line under the name) | Cards stay light; benefit beats spec | Card shows name + category + price + colour dots only; benefits live in the product story & PDP |
| Lots of white space, black type, imagery does the colour | Premium-but-casual tone | Warm "rice paper" neutral + GAMBOL red accent + deep lagoon for technology sections |
| Calm CTAs ("Shop", "Shop Now") | Friendly, never pushy | `SHOP NOW →`, `DISCOVER THE STORY`, Thai micro-copy for the human voice |

## 2. What belongs specifically to GAMBOL

* **Product reality** — GAMBOL is a Thai brand of flip-flops (หูหนีบ), slides (แตะสวม), sandals and sneakers. Real model codes on gambol.co.th follow `GM…` (men/unisex) and `GW…` (women), e.g. GM43111, GM11399, GW42174. Mock data reuses this naming.
* **Categories** — Men, Women, Kids, Sneakers (+ New Arrivals, Best Sellers).
* **Style lines** — the current site groups looks into **EZY, ZAH, ZAPP, ZEEK**. We keep these as "Collections" so existing merchandising carries over.
* **GBOLD Technology™** — the single biggest differentiator: *Comfort, Soft, Lite, Durable*. The current site buries it in a text page; the concept turns it into an interactive explorer on the homepage, the PDP and its own page.
* **Thai life context** — Bangkok sois, BTS commutes, weekend trips to the sea, temple-fair heat, rainy season. Copy is Thai-first with English display headlines (how Thai lifestyle brands actually speak).
* **Retail footprint** — sold in department stores and shoe shops nationwide; HQ in Samut Sakhon. Store locator gets first-class treatment (province → district search).
* **Content** — "Style" and "News & Activity" become **GAMBOL Stories** (editorial).
* **Brand marks** — the red **G** logo, the "JOIN THE WAY" graphic language seen on GM11397/GM11398 straps.

## 3. What must NOT be copied from REEF

* REEF wordmark, logo, iconography, surf/California positioning or any REEF copy.
* REEF's exact colour palette, fonts and button styles.
* Pixel-level layouts (hero composition, footer structure, card dimensions).
* REEF product names, collection names ("Weekend", "Rambler"…), apparel categories (GAMBOL doesn't sell apparel), clogs/boots categories.
* Surf culture imagery — GAMBOL's world is Thai city + sea + weekend travel, not surfing.

## 4. Gap analysis of the current gambol.co.th

| Issue | Concept response |
|---|---|
| Looks like a product catalogue / corporate site | Lifestyle-led hero + editorial rhythm |
| Technology is a static text page | Interactive hotspot explorer (homepage, PDP, /technology) |
| Discovery by gender only | Add discovery by lifestyle (Everyday, Travel, City Walk, Weekend, Outdoor, Relax) |
| Weak mobile experience | Mobile-first: bottom tab bar, horizontal scrollers, sticky add-to-cart |
| Store finder is a contact page | Province/district search + geolocation |
| Campaigns hardcoded | Every homepage section is data-driven (CMS schema) |
