# GAMBOL concept collection

## Register
brand

## Users
Thai footwear shoppers browsing on mobile and desktop, including men, women, parents, style-focused shoppers, and people prioritizing comfort. Brand stakeholders compare five original directions.

## Product Purpose
Five distinct homepage concepts and usable ecommerce prototypes for a Thai footwear brand. Make categories and products easy to discover, communicate comfort and innovation, and make campaign content maintainable by nontechnical staff.

## Brand Personality
Five explicitly requested voices: premium street; energetic sport; approachable lifestyle; intuitive commerce; precise innovation.

## Anti-references
Do not reproduce gambol.co.th's layout, typography, colors, components, or interactions. Do not reskin one template five times. No generic ecommerce aesthetic, science-fiction clichés, lorem ipsum, or live backend.

## Design Principles
- Each concept has an independent layout, hero, typography, discovery model, and interaction.
- Keep shared ecommerce tasks consistent and usable.
- Product imagery and people carry the brand story.
- Treat campaigns and sections as structured, reorderable content.
- Thai context, baht pricing, real copy; all product names and prices are illustrative.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA target; semantic markup, visible keyboard focus, accessible modal dialogs, reduced motion support, mobile bottom navigation, responsive imagery.

Source: the user's attached, detailed brief. This file records explicit requirements rather than inferred product strategy.
